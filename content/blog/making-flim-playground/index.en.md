It is the first end-to-end project I've been working on at my first job: idea conception, designing the architecture, user interface, and the user interactions, implementation, documentation and manuscript writing, and promotion. It may well be the template of my later projects and a life milestone/pointer that I will refer back from time to time. 

The process of making it come to life includes all the essential activities of philosophizing:

- Reflection
    - I've gone through several paradiagm shifts on FP's architecture, based on the ostensible contradition between users' needs and my own aesthetics (to be *simple*, *customizable*, and *frictionless*). Each shift require reflections on both parts to arrive at a broader architecture which gives me immense joy. See [History](https://github.com/skalalab/flim_playground/blob/main/HISTORY.md). 
    - Iterations of reflection are the key. It is extremely satisfying to look back and compare the past to the present and see how far FP has progressed, which exceeded my own expectation.
- Articulation
    - implement the code (the logic)
    - write the manuscript and documentation 
        - when writing abstracts and the bioarxiv paper, it sharpens my designs and makes it cleaner and simpler. 
    - give talks and workshops
- Argument
    - respond to reviewers' comments
    - respond to in-group critical suggestions
